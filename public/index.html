💡 Why Standalone Queries?
Vector databases (Chroma, Pinecone) and SQL search engines cannot understand pronouns or incomplete phrases like "What were the common reasons?".

Reformulating it to "What were the common reasons for customer billing escalations?" guarantees reliable search matches.
